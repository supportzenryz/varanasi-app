Private Dining Experiences

Creating Luxurious, Intimate
& Memorable Events

Here at Varanasi, we are proud to offer various private dining areas where customers and their guests can enjoy a fine dining experience away from the main restaurant. We currently have secluded spaces, sections or rooms within the restaurant available to book and ensure a memorable event is had.

Our private dining experiences are completely versatile and can be tailored to any occasion. Each private room has distinguished features and decors that offer the unique ambience and character of Varanasi.

  • Formal Corporate Dinners
  • Awards Ceremonies
  • Meetings and Conferences
  • Milestone Birthdays
  • Anniversaries and Proposals
  • Charity Fundraisers
  • Intimate Wedding Celebrations
