Corporate Dining Events

Private & Sophisticated Functions

As Leicester’s premier restaurant, spanning 6,000 sq ft with a capacity of 150 guests, Varanasi Boutique offers exceptional private and corporate dining experiences. Our expert team provides tailored corporate packages, perfect for staff celebrations, business meetings, and exclusive events.

Experience corporate fine dining like never before with our award-winning Indian cuisine. At Varanasi Boutique, we understand the importance of creating impactful corporate events. From elegant hospitality setups to large-scale dinners, our team specialises in delivering outstanding business events tailored to a diverse range of clients.

No event is too large or small. Whether you’re organising an intimate gourmet lunch, a memorable product launch, or a charity gala, our dedicated team will bring your vision to life. Varanasi Boutique provides a discreet, personalised, and entirely bespoke service. Our menus offer a unique culinary experience to suit all preferences and styles.
