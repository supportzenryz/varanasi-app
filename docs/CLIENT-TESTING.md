# Testing the new Varanasi website

Everything below happens on the test site. **No real card is ever charged and
no real customer is affected.** Use test card `4242 4242 4242 4242`, any future
expiry date, any three-digit security code.

The site: **varanasi-app-production.up.railway.app**

Please try to break it. If something confuses you for even a moment, that is
worth reporting — a guest deciding where to spend £200 will not persevere.

---

## 1. The front of house — about ten minutes

Open the site on your **phone** first. Most people will meet the restaurant
that way.

- Does the opening screen look right for Varanasi?
- Tap **Explore More**. Do the four menus look inviting?
- Open each menu. Are the prices right? Any dish missing, or one you no longer
  serve?
- Check **Drinks**, **Private Dining**, **Gallery** and **Contact**.
- At the bottom of any page: is the address, telephone number and are the
  opening hours correct? Is the other restaurant listed?

Then the same on a computer.

**Tell us:** anything wrong about the food, the prices, the photographs or the
wording. You know the restaurant; we do not.

---

## 2. Book a table — about ten minutes

1. Press **Book a table**.
2. Choose two guests. Look at the calendar: days you are closed should be
   crossed out. Hover one — it should say why.
3. Pick a date and a time.
4. Fill in your details and pay with the test card above.
5. You should land on a confirmation page with a booking reference, and
   **receive an email**.

Then try to break it:

- Book a table for **tonight, after 10pm**. It should move you to the next day
  and say why, not leave you staring at crossed-out times.
- Type nonsense into the telephone box. It should refuse it and say so.
- Press **Book** twice quickly. You should get one booking, not two.
- Cancel a booking from the link in your confirmation email. You and the
  restaurant should both be emailed.

**Tell us:** anywhere you were unsure what to do next, or where the site said
something you would not say to a guest.

---

## 3. The office — about fifteen minutes

Sign in at **varanasi-app-production.up.railway.app/admin** with the details
we have sent you separately. You will be asked to set your own password first.

Try the jobs you would really do:

- **Change a price** on a dish. Then look at the public menu — it should have
  changed.
- **Block a date** (Blocked dates). Then open the booking page as a customer:
  that day should now be crossed out and give your reason.
- **Log a phone booking** (Reservations). Try a date in the past — it should
  refuse.
- **Look up a gift voucher** and redeem part of it.
- **Add a member of staff**, set them to one restaurant, and check they only
  see that restaurant.
- **Activity log** — everything you have just done should be listed.

**Tell us:** any job you could not work out how to do, and anything that
happened without telling you what it had done.

---

## 4. What we would especially like you to check

- **The two restaurants stay separate.** A Leicester manager must never see
  Birmingham's bookings, and the reverse. Try hard to get across.
- **Nothing silently fails.** Every button should either do something or say
  why it did not.
- **The wording.** It is written in the restaurant's voice, and you are the
  authority on that.

---

## Reporting

For anything you find, we need three things:

1. What you were trying to do
2. What happened instead
3. Which device — phone or computer — and roughly what time

A photograph of the screen is worth more than a description. Please send them
as you go rather than saving them up.

---

## What is not being tested yet

- **Payments are in test mode.** No card is charged and no money moves.
- **Emails** may arrive from a temporary address until varanasi.uk is verified.
- **The web address** will change to varanasi.uk when we go live.
- **WhatsApp alerts to the floor** need Meta's approval of the message wording
  and are not switched on yet.
